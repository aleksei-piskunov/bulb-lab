Bulb Lab

A playground for experimenting with the puzzle below. Try different move sequences and grid sizes, and compare your successful attempts.

The puzzle

An 8 × 8 grid contains 64 light bulbs, all initially off. In one move, choose a cell and toggle every bulb in its row and column: an off bulb turns on, and an on bulb turns off. The selected bulb, where the row and column intersect, is toggled only once.

What is the minimum number of moves required to turn all 64 bulbs on?

The simulator starts with the original 8 × 8 grid. You can also explore other grid sizes using the same rule. Click a bulb to make a move.

Each dimension can be 1–30. New grid and Reset clear the current attempt; saved results are kept.
